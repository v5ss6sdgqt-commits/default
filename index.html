About Me

Capturing moments,
crafting stories.

Based in Portland, Oregon, I've spent the last decade chasing light across five continents. My work lives at the intersection of documentary honesty and fine-art composition — every frame a quiet conversation between subject and environment.

I shoot primarily with medium-format film and full-frame digital, believing that the right tool should be invisible, leaving only the image.
